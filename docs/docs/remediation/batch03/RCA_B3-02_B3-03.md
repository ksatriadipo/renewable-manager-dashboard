### Cause (file/function)
1. **Night lamp pools only exist on the hub plaza.** `NightLampPools` points are baked from `HomeDistrict`'s children (`HomeDistrict.cs:154-157` `Start` → `NightLampPools.Bake(gameObject)`; `NightLampPools.cs:37-39` `SubmitAll/LastCount/Registered`). The region 15× builder places lamp props as plain meshes (`RegionMapBuilder.cs:360` `"lamp" → TOWN-SOLAR-LAMP-V5_HC`) and registers **no** `NightLampPools` and no `_NightGlow` head for them. In the map space, glow discs need `MapDecor` (`MapDecor.NightLod.cs`), and `MapDecor` is null in 15× (ROOT_CAUSE_EVIDENCE_U37H G04 rank 3). Result: the HqSite apron and region plaza lamps stay dark at night. UNVERIFIED: whether the apron posts in 45/46 are HqSite children or region `lamp` props. The DX3 diagnostic (`QaDiagDx3.cs:95`) logs `night_lamp_pools_registered/last_count` but not per-owner.
2. **The wrong night grade space is used while the region is on screen.** `HubHUD.Home.cs:97-114`: `homeView = office && home != null && !_interior` → `home.enabled = homeView` (`:101`) and `NightGrade.Space = … homeView ? NightSpace.Plaza` (`:113-114`). `HomeDistrict.Update` (`HomeDistrict.cs:218-226`) then forces `NightGrade.Space = NightSpace.Plaza` every frame. In 15× the camera shows the region (`HubHUD.HomeCam15x.cs:37-48` `FrameHq15x`, `:61` `ApplyHomeCam15x`), so the region is graded with `PlazaMul` (`NightGrade.cs:31`, `:47` `MulFor`) instead of `MapMul`. It also bypasses the 19 Nov per-zoom night rule (FAR/MID/CLOSE, Lit/LitCount = truth), which lives in the Map path (`NaturePilot.cs:57`, `ElecWindows15x`). Peta and Beranda therefore grade the same houses differently.
3. **Capture precondition (tool side, G22):** `wn_plaza_night` aims at `Plaza` (hub, x≈2000) and correctly reports `offscreen`. This is a valid FAIL, not a product defect by itself. It must be re-pointed at whichever plaza the player sees (Auditor owns the tool).

## B3-03 — G17c decor placement (U2-034)

Scope guard: free furniture placement is deferred (user 15 Nov). The scope is "existing decor zones work in 15×", not new placement features.

### Symptom (evidence)
| Capture | What it shows |

The 8 map slots sit on a ring of `DecorMapRadiusKm` around `HqPos`, which at this zoom is either outside the frame or hidden (UNVERIFIED: no per-slot screen dump in b3r12u). The empty region plaza square with its grey fountain tile is visible to the right. |
| U37H tracker `MASTER_REMEDIATION_TRACKER_U37H.md:61` | U2-034 major PRODUCT, G17, PROBABLE, evidence u37h 67/68. |

### Cause (file/function)
1. **Plaza slots are computed on the hub plaza, which is off-screen in 15×.** `HubHUD.DecorPlace.cs:427-464` `DecorSpotWorld`: no hook (`DecorSlotWorld` is declared at `:20` but never assigned anywhere in the codebase), so for `DecorZones.Plaza` it uses `Plaza` = `home.GetComponent<BerandaPlaza>()` (`HubHUD.Plaza.cs:20-27`) → a ring of 4 + 8 points around the hub plaza extent at x≈2000. When `Plaza == null`, it falls back to one point `home.HomeFocus` (`:441-442`), which is also x≈2000.
2. **Markers are hidden because they project outside the viewport.** `UpdateDecorMode` (`HubHUD.DecorPlace.cs:472-521`) sets `visibility = Hidden` unless the projected rect is inside the layer bounds (`:516`). With the camera framed on the region campus (`EnterDecorMode` → `Select("office")`, `:90-94`; r533 `ApplyHomeCam15x`), all 12 plaza spots fall outside → 0 markers.
3. **Placed decor renders at the hub too.** `HubHUD.DecorWorld.cs:112-119` (`case DecorZones.Plaza`) uses the same `DecorSpotWorld`, so the "Terpasang di sini" bench (slot 0, placed by `QaDecorGarageSteps`, `:580`) is built at x≈2000, where the player never sees it.
4. **Map zone ("Sekitar HQ")**: `DecorSpotWorld` map ring (`:434-440`) uses `HqPos` (`HubHUD.Small.cs:98`, campus transform) and `DecorMapViewSize` (`:88`) from the hex-era map scale. Under 15×, `KmPerMapUnit` and region zoom limits differ, so ring radius and view size probably do not fit the region camera (UNVERIFIED; needs the DX3 per-slot log).
5. Zone tabs: the bar shows only Plaza + Sekitar HQ (`:183`); Office zone slots (`DecorOfficeSlotPose`, `DecorWorld.cs:90-95`) are reached only from the interior. Out of scope here, but shared with B3-01 interior.

Map slots = ring scaled to region units around the relocated campus. Scale `DecorPlazaScale` to 15× house size. Fits r37h criteria (fountain stays focus, nothing covers it). |

Beranda must then frame that tile, which contradicts r533 (frames the campus). |
